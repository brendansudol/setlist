Put the fills back into the intro

Watch the second half · 5:01
  1. Open-string pull-offs

    In bar 1, pick B1 with your index fretting it, then pull off to open B. Do the same from fret 2 on G and D with your middle finger. Finish by sliding your index on A from fret 1 into fret 2 of the next bar. Loop bar 1

  2. The two-string pull-off

    In bar 2, release the Bm7 grip and lay your index across B and high e at fret 3. Hammer from B3 to B5 with your ring finger. Flatten ring over both strings at fret 5, pick them together, then pull back to the index at fret 3. Loop bar 2

  3. Move the pair together

    At the end of bar 3, put index on G2 and middle on B3. Pick both, slide together to G4 and B5, then back to G2 and B3. Keep the same two fingers touching the strings throughout. Loop bar 3

  4. Slide into the F7 fragment

    In bar 4, place ring on G4, middle on B3 and pinky on high e4. Slide all three up one fret. You’ve landed on the top three notes of the F7/C diagram. Release for the open-string pull-offs. Loop bar 4
