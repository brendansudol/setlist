Playing the tab

Capo 1
Count frets from the capo. A 0 means an open string with the capo on. The G shape sounds A♭.
Fingerpicking
Pluck stacked frets together. H is a hammer-on, P is a pull-off, and an arrow connects the frets of a slide.
Strumming
Π marks a downstroke and V an upstroke. At an x, damp the strings for a short percussive hit.
Counting
Play gives you four clicks to count in. Count each beat “1 e & a” to place the quick fills and muted strokes.
