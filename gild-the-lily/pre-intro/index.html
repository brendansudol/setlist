How to read it

Lines
Six strings, high E at the top, low E at the bottom
Numbers
Frets. 0 is the open string.
Grey tails
How long each note lasts. Let it ring to the end of its tail.
H and P
Hammer-on and pull-off. The second note is not picked.
Underneath
The count, in eighths. Bold where a note starts.
Tuning

Bar by bar

It follows the chords of the verse, a bar each, so most of it is a chord shape held down and picked one string at a time.

  1. Bar 1DThe open D string under an open D shape. On beat 3, pick fret 2 of the high E and hammer on to fret 3, then let it ring through beat 4.
  2. Bar 2AmThe open A string under an Am shape. Every fretted note in the bar is already under your fingers if you hold the chord.
  3. Bar 3GThe bass moves to fret 3 of the low E. Over it the G string goes 2, 4, then 4 pulled off to 2, and 2 once more on the last half beat.
  4. Bar 4DTwo open D strings, a beat each, then the open D with fret 2 of the G string, held for two beats.
  5. Bar 5DTwo open D strings again, then the ending of bar 1: fret 2 of the high E hammered on to fret 3.
  6. Bar 6AmThe open A string with fret 1 of the B string, then fret 1 of the B string alone. Two beats each.
  7. Bar 7DTwo notes together, held for the whole bar: fret 5 of the A string and fret 4 of the D string. That is D with F# above it.

The chord names and the shapes are a reading of the notes. The lesson's tab does not name a chord over these bars; in every one the lowest note is the root of the chord given here.
