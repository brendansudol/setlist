Four kinds of shape cover all nine

  • Power-chord shape. The intro's G at fret 3 and F at fret 1 are one hand, two frets apart.
  • C barre shape. The intro's C and Csus4, one pinky apart.
  • Open shapes. Open C, and two versions of G with a single finger on the low E string.
  • Thumb-over F. Fmaj7 and F, both with the thumb fretting the bass note.

Reading the diagrams: the left line is the low E string and the right line is the high E. The thick line at the top is the nut; a label such as 3fr means the diagram starts at fret 3 instead. T is your thumb, wrapped over the top of the neck. Numbers are fingers, 1 for index through 4 for pinky. × marks a string that is not part of the shape and a ring marks an open string. The letters underneath are the notes that sound. Frets and strings are the ones called out in the lesson and checked against the video. Finger numbers follow the video where it shows them clearly and the usual fingering otherwise.

Where the hand sits, section by section

What the picking hand does in the verse

  • A plucked string. These three are the same in every verse bar.
  • A tune note on the B or high E string. Which string and fret changes from bar to bar.
  • The bass note is on the A string under C and on the low E string under G and Fmaj7.
  • The first three notes never change, so they are worth getting automatic before adding the tune.
  • The two tune notes are what the lesson teaches bar by bar. This page does not write them out; the verse section of the video does.
  • The intro is strummed, and the pre-chorus and chorus mix picking with light strums. Those are easier to watch than to read.
  • The lesson names strings, not picking-hand fingers, so the diagram does not assign any.

A practice order

  1. Slide the power-chord shape between G at fret 3 and F at fret 1 until it moves as one hand.
  2. Hold the C barre and add and lift the pinky for Csus4.
  3. Strum the intro slowly: a bar each of C, Csus4 and G, then the fourth bar that ends on F.
  4. Loop the verse chords with the bass note only: C, G, Fmaj7, G. Getting the thumb over for Fmaj7 is the hard part.
  5. Add the D and G strings after each bass note, then the two tune notes from the video, one bar at a time.
  6. For the pre-chorus and chorus, swap in the fuller G and F and follow the video for the strumming.
