# All The Small Things: how this page was made

**Live page:** https://brendansudol.github.io/setlist/all-the-small-things/

[`index.html`](index.html) is a one-file explainer for the nine fretting-hand
shapes in the intro, first verse, pre-chorus and chorus of a fingerstyle
arrangement of "All The Small Things", worked out from a YouTube guitar
lesson. It is generated by [`build.py`](build.py). This file records where the
content came from, how the page was produced, and what is and is not certain.

## Source

| | |
|---|---|
| Video | [All The Small Things (Blink 182) - Fingerstyle Guitar Lesson](https://youtu.be/hsBlzBTk9Xc) |
| Channel | Six String Fingerpicking |
| Length | 17:55 |
| Covers | Intro, first verse, pre-chorus and chorus. The instructor says another verse and a bridge follow and that he teaches those on his website. |
| Tuning | Standard. The lesson does not say so; see "How sure each part is". |
| Capo | None. The nut is clear in every frame. |

## Who made it

Claude (Anthropic's AI model) produced the page in the same working session
as [Your Song](../your-song/README.md), using the same pipeline.

## The prompt

The whole request for this page was:

> now do the same for another youtube lesson: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hsBlzBTk9Xc

"The same" refers to the two earlier songs in this repository and the rules in
the handoff note quoted from in
[The Heart of Life's write-up](../heart-of-life/README.md).

## Process

1. **Facts about the video.** Opened in Chrome. Title, channel, length,
   publish date and the chapter list in the description were read from
   `window.ytInitialPlayerResponse`.
2. **Transcript.** The "Show transcript" panel was opened and its 172
   segments read from the page. The instructor names every chord and most of
   the frets and strings out loud.
3. **Frames to confirm each shape.** The video was paused at about twenty
   moments across the four sections and the fretting hand inspected at 1080p. Fret
   wires were counted from the nut to read the fret, and the string was read
   from where the fingertip lands.
4. **Data, then a generator.** The shapes went into `build.py` as data. The
   script checks every shape before writing any HTML, then draws all the
   diagrams as inline SVG.
5. **Layout check.** The page was rendered at 390px wide in headless Chrome,
   in light and dark mode, section by section.

The frame-capture technique (playing a silent audio stream so a background
tab will load video, then drawing cropped frames onto a canvas in a labelled
grid) is described in
[Your Song's write-up](../your-song/README.md#getting-frames-from-a-background-tab).
It worked the same way here.

The frames and the transcript were working material only. Neither is in this
repository or on the page.

**The lead-in chords.** The lesson calls the two quick chords before bar 1 G5
and F5. The frames show the same hand as the intro's G and F, so the page
treats them as those two shapes hit on the low strings only, and gives them no
cards of their own.

**The verse figure.** The lesson counts each verse bar "one, two and three,
four": the bass note, the D string, the G string, then two notes on the top
two strings. The page draws that outline and marks the last two notes as
changing from bar to bar. It does not say which notes they are.

## How sure each part is

- **Confirmed by both the transcript and the frames:** the song map, the
  absence of a capo, and the frets and strings of all nine shapes.
- **Tuning is inferred.** The instructor never states it. The frets he names
  (fret 3 on the A string for C, fret 3 on the low E string for G, fret 1 for
  F) only give those chords in standard tuning, and the page says exactly
  that.
- **Finger numbers.** The thumb on the two F shapes is spoken and visible.
  The intro's C, G and F match the usual barre and power-chord fingering in
  the frames. For the two one-finger G shapes the frames show which strings
  are fretted but not reliably which fingers, so those numbers are the usual
  fingering. The page states the general rule.
- **The top string on the verse G.** The diagram marks it as outside the
  shape, because the first verse bars do not play it. Later bars do use it
  open, and the card says so.
- **Pre-chorus repeats.** The lesson gives the four bars and plays them, but
  does not say how many times they repeat, so the page does not either.

## Checks built into the generator

`build.py` refuses to write the page unless, for every shape:

- the notes produced by its strings and frets in standard tuning match the
  expected note list exactly;
- every note belongs to the chord the shape is named after;
- the lowest string in the shape gives the expected bass note;
- every fretted string has a finger, and no open or unused string does;
- any barre lies on strings in the shape and uses one finger;
- the shape fits in a four-fret diagram.

It also checks that each section is four bars, that every shape is used in at
least one section, and that all timestamps fall inside the video.

After the build, a separate pass confirmed that every in-page link has a
target.

## Rules the page follows

The same rules as the other songs:

- **No video frames.** Diagrams are original drawings, and every shape links
  to its moment in the video.
- **No lyrics, and no note-by-note melody or TAB.** The verse figure is shown
  as an outline only, and the strummed sections are pointed to rather than
  written out.
- **Fingering is labelled honestly**, as described above.
- **No embedded player**, so the page stays one file.

## Design

Same layout, type and colour rule as the other songs. What is specific here:

- **Three chord names, nine shapes.** Everything is a C, a G or an F, so the
  cards say where each version is used, and the song map links each bar to
  the right one.
- **Four families:** the power-chord shape (G, F), the C barre shape (C,
  Csus4), open shapes (open C and two one-finger Gs), and the thumb-over F
  (Fmaj7, F).
- **The hand chart is grouped by section**, which makes the main point
  visible: the intro is the only part played up the neck.
- **The picking grid is back**, as in The Heart of Life, because the verse has
  a repeating figure. Your Song had none.
- **The thick top line of a diagram is the nut.** There is no capo.

## Rebuilding

```bash
python3 all-the-small-things/build.py
```

Standard library only. Edit the data at the top of `build.py`, not
`index.html`; the next build overwrites the HTML.

## What is not in this repository

- The transcript and the frames used to confirm the shapes.
- The second verse and the bridge. They are not in this video.
